Before you merge

• Very large PDFs may use substantial device memory.

• Password-protected or damaged PDFs may not be supported.

• Form fields, bookmarks, annotations and digital signatures may not be preserved exactly.

• The page thumbnails load as needed to help performance on mobile.

Privacy note: PDF content is processed locally after libraries load. This page loads PDF libraries from public CDNs, so an internet connection is needed. It does not intentionally upload your documents to a server.
