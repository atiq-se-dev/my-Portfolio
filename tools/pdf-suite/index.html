Frequently asked questions

Is PageCraft PDF Studio free to use?

Yes. PageCraft PDF Studio is completely free and requires no signup or payment.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. All PDF processing runs locally in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your files never leave your device.

Can I merge multiple PDFs?

Yes. Upload two or more PDFs and use the Merge tool to combine them into a single document in your chosen order — use the ↑ / ↓ buttons next to each file to reorder.

Can I delete or extract specific pages?

Yes. Use the Extract or Delete tool and specify page ranges such as 1-3, 5, 8-10.

Does it work offline?

After the page and its PDF libraries load once, the tool works offline for most operations. Your files are never transmitted.
